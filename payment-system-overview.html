Payment Flow

1. Customer creates booking with payment_timing ('upfront' or 'post_service') 2. Payment record created with idempotency key (prevents duplicates) 3. Process via mock gateway (95% success, 5% random failure for testing) 4. Auto-create provider earning when payment confirmed 5. Auto-refund if provider cancels or customer cancels before provider accepts 6. Auto-payout provider earnings after service completion
